Mosaic Reveal: Pixel Memory

Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026 · Last updated 25 October 2026

Short version: Mosaic Reveal has no ads, no ad SDKs, no tracking and no accounts, and we do not sell any data. If the App crashes, an anonymous crash report is sent to Sentry so we can fix it. Anonymous gameplay statistics (GameAnalytics) are sent only if you say yes when the App asks, and you can switch them off in Settings at any time. Your progress stays on your device, plus optional cloud save and leaderboards through your own Apple / Google account. The full detail is below.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Mosaic Reveal: Pixel Memory ("the App", "we", "our") handles when you play it on iOS or Android. The App is developed by Jojogurt Games (Karol Kędziora, "the Developer"). For questions, email jojogurt.games@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

The App does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, photos, microphone, camera or contacts, and it does not create any user account. We do not run any servers that store player data. The only data that leaves your device for services we use are anonymous crash reports (§2.6) and, only with your consent, anonymous gameplay statistics (§2.7). Neither is linked to your identity or used for advertising.

1.1. Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress and preferences to a local file on your device:

  • Completed sectors and stars earned in each mosaic, and your star wallet
  • Daily mosaic state (last completed date, current streak)
  • Which mosaics you have unlocked (ownership is confirmed by the store)
  • Sound, music, haptics and language preferences
  • Your answer to the gameplay statistics question (§2.7)
  • Whether you have seen the tutorial

This data stays on your device unless you are signed in to Game Center / Google Play Games, in which case it is also synced as described in §2.2. Uninstalling the App deletes the local file.

2. Third-party and platform services

2.1. Advertising and tracking: none

Mosaic Reveal contains no advertising and no advertising SDKs, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA / GAID), and does not track you across apps or websites. Nothing the App sends is used for advertising, sold or shared with data brokers.

2.2. Game Center, Google Play Games and cloud save

Signing in to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android) is optional. If you are signed in:

  • Leaderboards: the App submits three whole numbers - your total stars, mosaics completed and daily streak - to Apple / Google, under their privacy policies. The platform identifies you with its own account; we never see your real name.
  • Cloud save: your progress (completed sectors, stars, daily streak and star wallet) is synced through your own iCloud (Game Center saved games) or Google Play Games saved games, so you can continue on another device. Apple / Google host this data on your behalf; we do not run a save server. You can delete it in your platform settings.

Apple Game Center · Google Play Games

2.3. Apple StoreKit and Google Play Billing (purchases)

Apple or Google processes every purchase (single mosaics and "All Mosaics"). We never see your card or account details - the App only receives whether a product is owned. Restoring purchases works the same way.

2.4. App Store / Play Store in-app review

If the App asks you to rate it, the prompt is presented by Apple or Google directly. We never see your review.

2.5. Daily mosaic date

To give everyone the same daily mosaic regardless of the device clock, the App requests today's date from a small Cloudflare Worker operated by us. The request contains no personal data, no identifiers and no body. Like any web request, the server sees your IP address; it does not store it.

2.6. Crash reports (Sentry)

If the App crashes or hits an unexpected error, it sends a crash report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), hosted in Sentry's EU (Germany) region, so we can find and fix the bug. A report contains the error and stack trace, a snapshot of the game's screen structure (no screenshots), the last lines of the App's log, the App version, and basic device information (device model, OS version, language, free memory). It contains no name, email, account or progress data. Sentry assigns a random installation ID so repeated crashes can be grouped; it is not linked to you. Like any web request, Sentry sees your IP address when the report is delivered; we do not use it to identify you. Crash reports are not used for analytics or advertising. Sentry is a US company: its infrastructure is in the United States and Germany, and it transfers European personal data under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, with the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (part of its Data Processing Addendum) as a fallback.

2.7. Gameplay statistics (GameAnalytics) - only with your consent

The first time you open the menu, the App asks whether we may collect anonymous gameplay statistics. Nothing is sent unless you choose "Sure", and you can change your answer at any time in Settings → Analytics. If you agree, the App sends anonymous events to GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics ApS, Denmark), for example: session start and length, which menu options are used, which screen you are on, and whether a purchase was started and completed (not what you paid or any payment details). GameAnalytics also records the App version, device model, OS version and the country derived from your IP address, and identifies your device with a random identifier it creates (on iOS the identifier for vendor, IDFV). It never uses the advertising identifier. We use these statistics only to balance the game and see where players get stuck. Switching Analytics off stops all further events.

3. Permissions the App requests

  • Internet / network state - for the daily mosaic date, leaderboards, cloud save, crash reports and (if you agreed) gameplay statistics.
  • Vibration / haptics (Android) - tactile feedback during play. You can turn it off in Settings.

The App does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, photo library, calendar, SMS or any other sensitive permission.

4. Children

Mosaic Reveal is a calm, non-violent puzzle game suitable for all ages. It has no ads, no accounts and no social features beyond optional platform leaderboards. It does not knowingly collect personal data from children: crash reports are anonymous, and gameplay statistics are sent only after the consent question is answered with "Sure". Purchases are handled by the store and can be restricted with the platform's parental controls.

5. Your rights and choices

We hold no account or profile about you. You can:

  • Turn gameplay statistics on or off - Settings → Analytics in the App.
  • Ask about or delete crash reports and statistics - email us; because they carry no name or account, tell us roughly when the crash happened and your device model so we can find them.
  • Reset your progress - delete the App from your device.
  • Delete cloud saves - iOS: Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage → Mosaic Reveal / Game Center. Android: Play Games → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data.
  • Stop leaderboard sharing - sign out of Game Center / Google Play Games.

If you are in the EU/EEA or the UK, you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict its processing, to data portability, and to object to processing based on our legitimate interest (the date request and crash reports, §2.5-2.6) - email us and we will stop it for your device. Where processing is based on consent (gameplay statistics), you can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Analytics; this does not affect processing before the withdrawal. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority - in Poland the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl), or the authority in your country of residence.

6. Data retention

  • On your device: until you uninstall the App.
  • iCloud / Play Games saved games and leaderboards: per Apple's and Google's policies, until you delete them.
  • Crash reports (Sentry): deleted automatically after at most 90 days.
  • Gameplay statistics (GameAnalytics): kept in aggregated and anonymous form under GameAnalytics' retention policy.
  • Our servers: we retain nothing.

7. Security

The local save file is signed to detect tampering. Cloud sync and leaderboards use Apple's and Google's own encrypted transports, and the date request, crash reports and gameplay statistics use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, but the only data of consequence here is your gameplay progress.

8. Notice for US state residents and international users

We do not sell or share personal information, do not use it for targeted advertising or profiling, and do not collect sensitive personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of. The platform services in §2 may process data outside your country under their own legal frameworks; for transfers by Sentry to the United States see §2.6. If you are in the EU/EEA, the date request and crash reports are based on our legitimate interest in operating the game and fixing errors (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); gameplay statistics are based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time in Settings; everything else is processed by Apple / Google under your agreement with them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top changes. Material changes are also announced in the App's update notes.

Każdy zakup (pojedyncze mozaiki i „Wszystkie mozaiki") obsługuje Apple lub Google. Nigdy nie widzimy danych karty ani konta — Aplikacja otrzymuje wyłącznie informację, czy dany produkt jest posiadany. Przywracanie zakupów działa tak samo.

Aby każdy gracz dostawał tę samą codzienną mozaikę niezależnie od zegara urządzenia, Aplikacja pobiera dzisiejszą datę z niewielkiego Cloudflare Workera prowadzonego przez nas. 2.6. Raporty awarii (Sentry)

Gdy Aplikacja ulegnie awarii lub napotka nieoczekiwany błąd, wysyła raport do Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), przechowywany w regionie UE (Niemcy), abyśmy mogli znaleźć i naprawić błąd. Raport zawiera opis błędu i ślad stosu, migawkę struktury ekranu gry (bez zrzutów ekranu), ostatnie linie dziennika Aplikacji, wersję Aplikacji oraz podstawowe dane urządzenia (model, wersja systemu, język, wolna pamięć). Nie zawiera imienia, e-maila, konta ani danych o postępie. Sentry nadaje losowy identyfikator instalacji, aby grupować powtarzające się awarie; nie jest on powiązany z Tobą. Jak przy każdym zapytaniu internetowym, Sentry widzi Twój adres IP przy dostarczeniu raportu; nie używamy go do identyfikacji. Raporty awarii nie służą do analityki ani reklam. Sentry to firma z USA: jej infrastruktura znajduje się w Stanach Zjednoczonych i w Niemczech, a dane osobowe z Europy przekazuje na podstawie programu EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, a pomocniczo standardowych klauzul umownych Komisji Europejskiej (część jej umowy powierzenia przetwarzania danych).

2.7. Statystyki rozgrywki (GameAnalytics) — tylko za Twoją zgodą

Przy pierwszym wejściu do menu Aplikacja pyta, czy możemy zbierać anonimowe statystyki rozgrywki. Nic nie jest wysyłane, dopóki nie wybierzesz „Jasne”, a odpowiedź możesz w każdej chwili zmienić w Ustawieniach → Statystyki. Jeśli się zgodzisz, Aplikacja wysyła anonimowe zdarzenia do GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics ApS, Dania), na przykład: początek i długość sesji, używane opcje menu, bieżący ekran oraz to, czy zakup został rozpoczęty i zakończony (bez kwot i danych płatności). GameAnalytics zapisuje też wersję Aplikacji, model urządzenia, wersję systemu i kraj ustalony na podstawie adresu IP, a urządzenie rozpoznaje po utworzonym przez siebie losowym identyfikatorze (na iOS: identyfikator dostawcy, IDFV). Nigdy nie używa identyfikatora reklamowego. Statystyk używamy wyłącznie do balansowania gry i sprawdzania, gdzie gracze się zacinają. Wyłączenie Statystyk zatrzymuje wysyłanie kolejnych zdarzeń.
