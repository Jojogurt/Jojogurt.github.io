Mosaic Reveal: Pixel Memory

Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026 · Last updated 24 October 2026

Short version: Mosaic Reveal has no ads, no ad SDKs, no tracking and no accounts, and we do not sell any data. We do not collect analytics or crash data. Your progress stays on your device, plus optional cloud save and leaderboards through your own Apple / Google account. The full detail is below.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Mosaic Reveal: Pixel Memory ("the App", "we", "our") handles when you play it on iOS or Android. The App is developed by Jojogurt Games (Karol Kędziora, "the Developer"). For questions, email jojogurt.games@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

We collect no personal data. The App does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, photos, microphone, camera or contacts, and it does not create any user account on our servers. We do not run any servers that store player data.

1.1. Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress and preferences to a local file on your device:

  • Completed sectors and stars earned in each mosaic, and your star wallet
  • Daily mosaic state (last completed date, current streak)
  • Which mosaics you have unlocked (ownership is confirmed by the store)
  • Sound, music, haptics and language preferences
  • Whether you have seen the tutorial

This data stays on your device unless you are signed in to Game Center / Google Play Games, in which case it is also synced as described in §2.2. Uninstalling the App deletes the local file.

2. Third-party and platform services

2.1. Advertising, tracking and analytics: none

Mosaic Reveal contains no advertising and no advertising SDKs, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA / GAID), and does not track you across apps or websites. The App's code includes optional GameAnalytics (analytics) and Sentry (crash reporting) integrations, but they are disabled in this version: analytics would only start after an explicit consent that the App never asks for, and crash reporting has no destination configured. No analytics or crash data is collected or sent anywhere. If this ever changes, this policy will be updated first and the App will ask for your consent.

2.2. Game Center, Google Play Games and cloud save

Signing in to Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android) is optional. If you are signed in:

  • Leaderboards: the App submits three whole numbers - your total stars, mosaics completed and daily streak - to Apple / Google, under their privacy policies. The platform identifies you with its own account; we never see your real name.
  • Cloud save: your progress (completed sectors, stars, daily streak and star wallet) is synced through your own iCloud (Game Center saved games) or Google Play Games saved games, so you can continue on another device. Apple / Google host this data on your behalf; we do not run a save server. You can delete it in your platform settings.

Apple Game Center · Google Play Games

2.3. Apple StoreKit and Google Play Billing (purchases)

Apple or Google processes every purchase (single mosaics and "All Mosaics"). We never see your card or account details - the App only receives whether a product is owned. Restoring purchases works the same way.

2.4. App Store / Play Store in-app review

If the App asks you to rate it, the prompt is presented by Apple or Google directly. We never see your review.

2.5. Daily mosaic date

To give everyone the same daily mosaic regardless of the device clock, the App requests today's date from a small Cloudflare Worker operated by us. The request contains no personal data, no identifiers and no body. Like any web request, the server sees your IP address; it does not store it.

3. Permissions the App requests

  • Internet / network state - for the daily mosaic date, leaderboards and cloud save.
  • Vibration / haptics (Android) - tactile feedback during play. You can turn it off in Settings.

The App does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, photo library, calendar, SMS or any other sensitive permission.

4. Children

Mosaic Reveal is a calm, non-violent puzzle game suitable for all ages. It has no ads, no accounts and no social features beyond optional platform leaderboards, and it does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. Purchases are handled by the store and can be restricted with the platform's parental controls.

5. Your rights and choices

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing on our side to access, correct or delete. You can:

  • Reset your progress - delete the App from your device.
  • Delete cloud saves - iOS: Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage → Mosaic Reveal / Game Center. Android: Play Games → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data.
  • Stop leaderboard sharing - sign out of Game Center / Google Play Games.

6. Data retention

  • On your device: until you uninstall the App.
  • iCloud / Play Games saved games and leaderboards: per Apple's and Google's policies, until you delete them.
  • Our servers: we retain nothing.

7. Security

The local save file is signed to detect tampering. Cloud sync and leaderboards use Apple's and Google's own encrypted transports, and the date request uses HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, but the only data of consequence here is your gameplay progress.

8. Notice for US state residents and international users

We do not sell or share personal information, do not use it for targeted advertising or profiling, and do not collect sensitive personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of. The platform services in §2 may process data outside your country under their own legal frameworks. If you are in the EU/EEA, the limited processing described above (the date request) is based on our legitimate interest in operating the game; everything else is processed by Apple / Google under your agreement with them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top changes. Material changes are also announced in the App's update notes.

2. Usługi zewnętrzne i platformowe

2.1. Reklamy, śledzenie i analityka: brak

Mosaic Reveal nie zawiera reklam ani SDK reklamowych, nie korzysta z identyfikatora reklamowego (IDFA / GAID) i nie śledzi Cię w innych aplikacjach ani witrynach. Kod Aplikacji zawiera opcjonalne integracje GameAnalytics (analityka) i Sentry (raporty awarii), ale w tej wersji są wyłączone: analityka uruchomiłaby się dopiero po wyraźnej zgodzie, o którą Aplikacja nigdy nie prosi, a raportowanie awarii nie ma skonfigurowanego adresu docelowego. Nie zbieramy ani nie wysyłamy nigdzie danych analitycznych ani o awariach. Jeśli to się zmieni, najpierw zaktualizujemy tę politykę, a Aplikacja poprosi o Twoją zgodę.

Logowanie do Game Center (iOS) lub Google Play Games (Android) jest opcjonalne. Jeśli jesteś zalogowany:

  • Zapis w chmurze: Twój postęp (ukończone sektory, gwiazdki, seria dzienna i zapas gwiazdek) jest synchronizowany przez Twoje własne iCloud (zapisane gry Game Center) lub zapisane gry Google Play Games, aby można było kontynuować na innym urządzeniu. Dane przechowują Apple / Google; nie prowadzimy serwera zapisów. Możesz je usunąć w ustawieniach platformy.

Każdy zakup (pojedyncze mozaiki i „Wszystkie mozaiki") obsługuje Apple lub Google. Nigdy nie widzimy danych karty ani konta — Aplikacja otrzymuje wyłącznie informację, czy dany produkt jest posiadany. Przywracanie zakupów działa tak samo.

2.4. Ocena Aplikacji w App Store / Google Play

Jeśli Aplikacja poprosi o ocenę, okno wyświetla bezpośrednio Apple lub Google. Nigdy nie widzimy Twojej recenzji.

2.5. Data codziennej mozaiki

Aby każdy gracz dostawał tę samą codzienną mozaikę niezależnie od zegara urządzenia, Aplikacja pobiera dzisiejszą datę z niewielkiego Cloudflare Workera prowadzonego przez nas. Zapytanie nie zawiera danych osobowych, identyfikatorów ani treści. Jak każde zapytanie internetowe, serwer widzi Twój adres IP, ale go nie zapisuje.

3. Uprawnienia, o które prosi Aplikacja
