2. The service

NextLeg is a free iPhone app (iOS 26 or later) that uses your activity from Apple Health to show personal and group daily goals, a group streak, weekly challenges and rankings, and lets you chat with friends in a group and send cheers and nudges. You must be at least 13 years old.

Your account is created automatically on first launch and is anonymous. You can secure it with Sign in with Apple so you don’t lose it when you change phones. You’re responsible for what happens on your account. Please report your activity honestly — don’t try to fake it (manually entered Health data is ignored and unrealistic values are capped).

You also must not try to bypass the app’s protections, overload our servers, or otherwise disrupt the service or other people’s use of it.

5. Reporting and blocking

You can report any message, cheer or person (long-press a message, or open a person’s profile and use the “…” menu), and any workout photo (the “…” menu on the photo). A reported photo disappears for you at once, and for the whole group once the group creator or two different members report it. The group creator can delete any photo in their group. You can also block a user so you no longer see their content. We review reports within 24 hours and remove content and accounts where needed. The automatic filter masks some offensive words, but it isn’t perfect — so we count on your reports too.

6. Content you add

What you create (name, photos, messages) remains yours. You give us permission to store it and show it to members of your groups as needed to run the app. You’re responsible for having the right to share it and for keeping it within section 4.

Workout photos are visible only to members of the group you add them to and are deleted automatically after 7 days. Only add photos you have the right to share, and don’t show other people in them without their consent. Before upload we downscale the photo and strip its metadata (EXIF, GPS location).

NextLeg is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Goals, streaks and rankings are there to motivate you, not to diagnose or treat anything. Talk to a doctor before starting or increasing physical activity, especially if you have a health condition or any doubts. You exercise at your own risk; listen to your body and never push beyond your limits to “keep a streak”.
