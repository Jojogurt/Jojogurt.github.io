Gameplay

Features

Predict the landing order

Watch the slow-motion preview, then guess which items hit the floor first. Real physics — feathers float, anvils drop.

10 themed worlds

Animals, fruits, vegetables, household, sport, tools, sweets, farm, dinosaurs and construction.

Freeze & hint skills

Slow down time or ask for a hint when the items are flying fast — just enough help to keep it fair.

Picture collection

Every correctly-guessed item joins your pokedex-style collection — fun to come back and admire what you've gathered.

Ad-free, kid-safe

No ads, no logins, no chat, no interrupting purchases. Designed for ages 4+ with big icons for little fingers.

16 achievements and a high-score leaderboard to share with the family — optional and opt-in.
