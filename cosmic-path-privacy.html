Cosmic Path

Privacy Policy

Effective 18 May 2026 · Last updated 1 June 2026

Short version: Cosmic Path uses standard mobile SDKs (AdMob, GameAnalytics, Sentry) and the platform's own cloud-save / leaderboard services. We don't run our own user accounts or servers, and we never see your name, email, payment information, or anything else that personally identifies you. The full detail is below.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Cosmic Path ("the App", "we", "our") handles when you play it on iOS or Android. The App is developed by Karol Kędziora ("the Developer"). For questions, email kedziora.karol@gmail.com.

1. What we collect

We designed Cosmic Path to collect as little personal data as possible. The App itself does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, photos, microphone, camera, or contacts. It does not create or store any user account on our own servers.

What is processed falls into two categories.

1.1. Data stored on your device only

The game saves your progress and preferences to a local file (save.json) on your device:

  • Current and highest level reached
  • Score from the current run
  • Audio, music, and haptics preferences
  • Whether you've finished the tutorial
  • Number of times you've launched the game
  • Daily-challenge state (last completed date, current streak)
  • Skill charge counts
  • Relax-mode parameters (grid size, path length, speed)
  • Whether you've purchased "Remove Ads"
  • Whether cloud sync is enabled

This data never leaves your device unless cloud sync is on (see §2.4). Uninstalling the App deletes this file.

1.2. Data processed by third-party services

The App integrates a small number of third-party SDKs and platform services described in §2. Each handles its own data flow under its own privacy policy — we don't add anything to what they collect by default.

2. Third-party services

2.0. Consent (EEA / iOS)

On first launch — and again any time you change your mind — Cosmic Path collects your consent for advertising and analytics:

  • EEA (including Poland): a Google UMP form asks whether you agree to personalized ads and related processing. If you decline, AdMob serves non-personalized ads only and GameAnalytics events are not transmitted for that session.
  • iOS (all regions): Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks for permission to use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). Tapping Ask App Not to Track disables personalized ads regardless of the UMP choice.

Withdraw or change your consent at any time: open Settings inside the App and tap Privacy Options — the UMP form re-appears with your current choice. iOS users can additionally manage tracking system-wide under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

2.1. Google AdMob (advertising)

For free players, the App shows ads delivered by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android — only with your OS-level consent) and standard ad-request data (IP address, device model, OS version, language, ad interactions). You can purchase "Remove Ads" to remove banners and interstitials.

Google's policies: policies.google.com/privacy and support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

2.2. GameAnalytics (gameplay analytics)

The App sends anonymous gameplay events to GameAnalytics — for example: level started / completed / failed, skills used, daily-challenge completion, tutorial progress, IAP started / succeeded, menu actions, session start and end. No name, email, or device identifier is attached. GameAnalytics helps us understand game balance and player flow.

Privacy policy: gameanalytics.com/privacy.

2.3. Sentry (crash reports)

If the App crashes, Sentry receives a stack trace and the scene structure at the moment of the crash so we can fix bugs. Crash reports may incidentally include log messages but no personal information is attached (no name, email, screenshots). We do not link crash reports to any specific user.

Privacy policy: sentry.io/privacy.

2.4. iCloud and Play Games Saved Games (cloud sync)

If cloud sync is enabled (Settings → cloud sync row), the App backs up your save.json to your own iCloud account (iOS) or Play Games Saved Games slot (Android) so you can continue progress across devices. Apple / Google host this data on your behalf — we do not run our own save server. You can delete cloud data at any time via your platform settings.

Cloud sync excludes the "Remove Ads" flag (the store handles that directly) and the cloud-sync toggle itself.

2.5. Game Center and Play Games (leaderboards & achievements)

If you sign in to Game Center / Play Games, your scores and unlocked achievements are submitted to those services so you appear on leaderboards. The submitted data is integer values only (level reached, score, streak). The platform identifies you using its own account ID — we never see your real name unless you've made your platform profile public.

Apple Game Center · Google Play Games

2.6. Apple StoreKit and Google Play Billing (purchases)

If you buy "Remove Ads", Apple or Google handles the entire transaction. We never see your credit card or account information — we only receive a confirmation that the purchase succeeded for the product ID com.kked.games.cosmicPath.adFree. Restoring a purchase works the same way.

2.7. App Store / Play Store in-app review

If we ask you to rate the App, the rating prompt is presented by Apple or Google directly. We never see your review content; you submit it to the store, not to us.

2.8. Daily-challenge server time

Once per launch, the App makes a single HTTPS request to a small Cloudflare Worker (https://raspy-poetry-6c03.kedziora-karol.workers.dev/) that returns today's UTC date. This ensures everyone gets the same Daily Challenge regardless of their device clock or timezone. The request contains no personal data, no identifiers, and no body — only standard HTTP metadata such as your IP address, which we don't log persistently.

3. Permissions the App requests

  • Internet / network state — required for ads, cloud sync, leaderboards, analytics, and the daily-challenge date check.
  • Vibration / haptics (Android) — tactile feedback on button taps and gameplay events. Disable in Settings.

The App does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, photo library, calendar, SMS, or any other sensitive permission.

4. Children

Cosmic Path is a non-violent puzzle game suitable for general audiences but is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used the App, simply delete the App and any local progress is removed.

We do not currently enable "child-directed treatment" on AdMob. If you are publishing the App in a market that requires COPPA / GDPR-K compliance, please reach out before installing.

5. Your rights and choices

Because we don't collect personal data ourselves, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, or delete. You can, however:

  • Withdraw or change consent — open the App, go to Settings → Privacy Options. The consent form re-opens with your current choice. Withdrawing also stops analytics events from being sent for the rest of the session.
  • Reset your progress — delete the App from your device.
  • Delete cloud saves — iOS: Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage → Cosmic Path. Android: Play Games → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data.
  • Reset your advertising identifier — iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Android: Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Opt out of personalized ads — see Google's instructions at support.google.com/My-Ad-Center-Help.
  • Stop crash reports & analytics — uninstalling the App stops all data collection going forward.

For requests related to GameAnalytics or Sentry data tied to your device, contact those providers directly using the links in §2 — we don't have a way to identify you on their side, since we don't tag events with personal identifiers.

6. Data retention

  • On your device: until you uninstall the App.
  • iCloud / Play Games Saved Games: until you delete it via your platform settings.
  • GameAnalytics: per GameAnalytics' retention schedule (typically 12–24 months for raw events).
  • Sentry: per Sentry's retention schedule (typically 30–90 days on free tiers).
  • AdMob: per Google's policies.

7. Security

The local save file is signed with an HMAC to detect tampering. Cloud syncs use Apple and Google's own encrypted transports. Third-party SDKs operate over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, but the only data of consequence here is your gameplay progress.

8. Notice for US state residents

If you reside in a US state with a comprehensive privacy law — including California (CCPA / CPRA), Virginia (VCDPA), Colorado (CPA), Connecticut (CTDPA), Utah (UCPA), Texas (TDPSA), Oregon (OCPA), Montana (MCDPA), Iowa (ICDPA), and Delaware (DPDPA) — you have additional rights regarding personal information processed about you.

Cosmic Path does not run its own user accounts or servers, so on our side there is no personal information to access, correct, port, or delete. What is processed flows through the third-party SDKs and platform services listed in §2 — primarily AdMob for advertising and GameAnalytics for anonymous gameplay metrics.

"Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" / Opt out of targeted advertising. Open the App and tap Settings → Privacy Options. The form that appears is the same one we use for EEA consent — it is provided by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) and automatically presents the appropriate state-specific opt-out controls based on your region. Disabling personalization there stops AdMob from "selling" or "sharing" data for targeted advertising within the meaning of these state laws.

Categories of personal information potentially processed by our partners include device identifiers (IDFA / GAID, only with your OS-level consent), IP address, device model, OS version, language, and aggregate ad/interaction data. We do not knowingly sell, share, or use sensitive personal information, and we do not engage in profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

If you have questions about a request under your state's privacy law, reach out via the contact email in §10. Because we don't tag events with personal identifiers on our side, requests directed at the providers (AdMob, GameAnalytics, Sentry) are best made via the links in §2.

9. International users

Cosmic Path is available worldwide. The third-party services we use may transfer data outside your country (e.g. to the US, EU, or Asia depending on the provider). By using the App, you acknowledge these transfers occur under the providers' own legal frameworks.

If you are in the EU/EEA: our legal basis for the limited processing described above is our legitimate interest in operating, improving, and monetizing the game, supplemented by your consent for advertising and analytics collected via the UMP form (§2.0). You can object at any time by uninstalling the App or withdrawing consent via Settings → Privacy Options.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the "Last updated" date at the top changes. Material changes are also announced in the App's update notes.

Możesz w każdej chwili wnieść sprzeciw, odinstalowując Aplikację lub wycofując zgodę w Ustawienia → Prywatność.
