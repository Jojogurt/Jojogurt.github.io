Gameplay

Features

Campaign in three acts

Learn each rule, combine them, then master them all: a decoy comet, paths traced backwards, lights out and total darkness - each with a short lesson and a free try.

Portals and boss levels

The comet dives into one portal and comes out of another. Every few levels a boss mixes the rules into one long path - beat it for 10 stars.

4 skills

Slow Time, Trail, Hint and Checkpoint. Slow Time, Trail and Checkpoint last 3 rounds - refill them with the stars you collect on the path.

Daily Challenge

5 rounds with a new twist for every day of the week, the same for every player. Keep your streak alive.

Relax mode

Unlocks at level 15. Choose the grid, path length, speed and rules - no pressure, no internet needed.

Game Center & Play Games

3 leaderboards, 10 achievements and cloud save. In 7 languages: English, Polish, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Italian.
