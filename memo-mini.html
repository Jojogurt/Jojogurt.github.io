Gameplay

Features

14 colorful worlds

Animals, farm, ocean, dinosaurs, instruments, happy numbers and more - each with its own painted world and pictures to match.

175 pictures to collect

Every new pair flies into the collection, and after each level you see the cards you have just discovered.

Learn words in 7 languages

Optional Educational Features: names on cards, reading and picture-word matching in English, Polish, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Italian.

Read aloud and real sounds

The name of every matched pair is spoken, and many pictures - farm animals, instruments, sea creatures - make their own sounds.

Grows with your child

Easy, Medium and Hard - from 4 cards to 24, gentle automatic hints and up to 3 stars per level.

Ad-free, kid-safe

No ads, no tracking, no text input, no timers, no harsh fail states. Purchases sit behind a parental gate.
