Gameplay

Features

Sector by sector

Each picture is a grid of tiles split into sectors. A sector flashes - tap its tiles back from memory, in any order. 3 lives per sector, 1-3 stars for how cleanly you do it.

Stars and checkpoints

Hints cost stars. Every 10 sectors there is a checkpoint: run out of lives and you go back to it - or spend 25 stars to keep going.

23 hand-made mosaics

A satellite, Saturn, a space cat, Jupiter, a comet, a black hole, an astronaut, a crescent moon, an alien, a rover, a nebula, a flying saucer, a space station, the Great Bear constellation and more.

Daily mosaic

A new mosaic every day, the same for every player. Keep your streak alive.

Fair and calm

Free to download: the first 5 mosaics and the daily one are free. Every other mosaic is a one-time purchase ($0.99), or "All Mosaics" ($4.99) unlocks every current and future one. No ads, no subscriptions, no energy or timers.

Game Center & Play Games

Leaderboards for total stars, mosaics completed and daily streak, plus cloud save. In 7 languages: English, Polish, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese (BR) and Italian.
